Your account

Provide accurate information, protect your login credentials, and use only accounts you are authorized to access. You are responsible for activity under your account. PushCircle’s current account setup is designed for adults. Notify us if you suspect unauthorized access.

Habits, pods, and proof

PushCircle is an accountability tool. It does not provide medical, mental-health, legal, or professional advice and does not guarantee habit outcomes. Choose safe, lawful goals. Joining a pod does not assign a habit automatically; a habit must be assigned before checking in. Pod owners and administrators can manage participation and available communication or proof settings.

Proof review and automated status changes are app features, not independent verification of real-world events. Pending proof may be approved automatically after the review timeout. Do not rely on PushCircle for emergencies or time-critical care.

You must have the rights and permission to share any images or other content you submit.

Use the in-app reporting and blocking tools or contact ternarylabsin@gmail.com about abuse. Pod administrators can moderate supported pod surfaces; platform operators handle reports concerning private content.
