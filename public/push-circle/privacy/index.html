PushCircle Privacy Policy

How Ternary Labs handles information in PushCircle.

Version: 2026-11-12 · Updated November 12, 2026

Who operates PushCircle

Ternary Labs operates PushCircle. Contact ternarylabsin@gmail.com with privacy questions or requests. This policy covers the PushCircle app and its supporting service.

Information we process

  • Account and profile: email address, authentication credentials handled by our authentication provider, display name, optional profile image, age band, timezone, and profile preferences such as gender label, lifestyle tags, and accountability intensity.
  • Accountability activity: habits, schedules, pod memberships, invitations, check-ins, notes, progress, structured pushes, and notification preferences.
  • Content you submit: proof photos, messages in enabled pod channels, abuse reports, and block relationships.
  • Device and operational information: push tokens, platform, app/build version, device name where available, request/network information, and service logs used to operate and protect the service.
  • Legal records: the versions of the Terms and Privacy Policy accepted by your account and the acceptance timestamp.

How information is used

We use this information to authenticate you, display your profile and activity to authorized users, operate habits and pods, deliver requested notifications, enforce feature limits, review abuse reports, protect the service, answer support requests, and meet applicable legal obligations. Habit entries and proof images may contain sensitive information you choose to share; avoid including unnecessary personal or third-party information.

Who can see your information

Your profile and accountability activity are shown according to your pod memberships and visibility settings. Proof photos are available to the submitter and authorized reviewers; approved proof can be shared with active pod members when the pod’s public-proof setting is enabled. Community Chat is visible to authorized pod members. Direct messages and owner threads are restricted to their participants, with platform access for support and safety. A pod owner does not automatically have access to other members’ private direct messages.

Reports are available to the platform for investigation. We use service providers including Supabase for authentication, database and storage, and Expo and Apple/Google push services for notifications. Notifications may display on your lock screen depending on device settings. Information may also be disclosed when required by law or necessary to investigate abuse, protect rights, or secure the service.

Retention and deletion

Account data is retained while needed to operate your account and provide the service. The Free plan’s current-month history and paid plans’ 90-day history are display/access windows; they are not promises that all underlying records are deleted after that period.

Proof images are scheduled to expire after 24 hours, with periodic cleanup; review records can remain after an image expires. Replaced or deleted proof may remain in restricted archives. Message retention follows pod settings and uses soft deletion. Operational records, safety reports, restricted archives and backups may remain where needed for security, legal obligations, or recovery. Contact us for requests concerning retained information.

You can initiate account deletion in the app from Profile. This removes the account and its associated service records through the account-deletion workflow. Owned pods are also removed, affecting their members. See account deletion instructions if you cannot sign in.

Your choices

You can edit profile information, manage habit visibility and notification preferences, deny photo or notification permissions, block/report users, and delete your account. Contact ternarylabsin@gmail.com to request access, correction, or deletion of personal information, or to exercise privacy rights available in your jurisdiction. We may need to verify that a request concerns your account.

Security and international processing

We use access controls to restrict data to authorized users and service operators. No system can guarantee absolute security. Our service providers may process information outside your country; applicable protections and rights depend on your location and the processing involved.

Children

PushCircle’s current account setup is designed for adults. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can investigate and address it.

Changes

We will update this page when the policy changes. The app records document versions and may ask you to review updated documents before continuing.
