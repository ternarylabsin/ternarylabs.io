PushCircle Support

Help with your account, pods, check-ins, and privacy.

Contact Ternary Labs

Email ternarylabsin@gmail.com. Include your app version, device type, and a description of what happened. Never send your password, authentication codes, or unnecessary proof photos.

Delete your account

  1. Open PushCircle and sign in.
  2. Open the Profile tab and choose Delete Account.
  3. Read the confirmation and confirm deletion.

Account deletion removes your account through the service’s deletion workflow. Pods you own are also removed, which affects other members. History display windows and media expiration differ from account deletion; see the Privacy Policy for retention details.

If you cannot sign in, email ternarylabsin@gmail.com with the subject “PushCircle account deletion” from the email associated with your account. We may verify ownership before acting. Do not send your password.

Can’t check in?

Join or create a pod, then assign an active habit to it using Assign Habit. If the pod requires proof for Made Goal, attach an eligible image under 5 MB. Pending proof protects the day while awaiting review; approval grants completion credit.

Missing reminders?

Check PushCircle’s notification preferences and your device’s notification permissions. Pod reminders require an assigned habit and an active membership. Network conditions and operating-system settings can affect delivery.

Report a safety concern

Use Report on the relevant user or content in the app. Blocking prevents supported direct interactions. You can also email ternarylabsin@gmail.com with the relevant pod or content details. PushCircle is not an emergency service.

Plans and billing

Paid checkout is not currently available. Listed Premium and Pod Pro prices are planned pricing, and access during beta is managed by Ternary Labs.
